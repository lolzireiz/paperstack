# Paperstack

Paper-trading practice app laid out like a brokerage app. Practice money only; not affiliated with any broker.

- Live U.S. stock prices from [Finnhub](https://finnhub.io) (free key, entered in the app's Settings and stored only on your device)
- Crypto prices from [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com)
- Market, limit, stop loss and stop limit orders; Day and GTC
- Individual + Crypto accounts, transfers, 1% crypto fee
- Trade journal, automatic investments, iCloud backup and restore

Open https://lolzireiz.github.io/paperstack/ in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.

To ship an update, bump `VERSION` in `index.html` and the number in `version.json` together; the app shows an "Update now" banner.
